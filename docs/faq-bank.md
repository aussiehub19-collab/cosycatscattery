## Homepage FAQ (6–8 questions — buying, pricing, shipping, product type only)

1. **How much does a Maine Coon kitten cost in Australia?** *(pricing)*
   Maine Coon kittens from a registered Australian cattery typically range from $1,800–$5,000+ depending on colour, pedigree, and whether desexing and full health testing are included. Cosy Cats Cattery's current available kittens are listed with exact pricing on our [Shop page](/shop/).

2. **How do I reserve a kitten from Cosy Cats Cattery?** *(buying)*
   Browse our [available kittens](/shop/), add your choice to your reservation cart, and complete the checkout form with your details. Our concierge confirms your reservation and organises delivery or pickup — see our [Checkout page](/checkout/) for the process.

3. **Do you deliver Maine Coon kittens Australia-wide?** *(shipping)*
   Yes. We arrange nationwide flight delivery to major Australian cities, plus in-person pickup from our Canberra estate by appointment. See our [Contact page](/contact/) for delivery to your specific location.

4. **Are your kittens DNA tested and health guaranteed?** *(product type)*
   Yes — every kitten is DNA-tested for HCM, SMA, and PKDef, and comes with a genetic health guarantee and full pedigree documentation. Read our [DNA testing guide](/blog/understanding-maine-coon-dna-testing-hcm-sma-pkdef/) for what these tests actually check.

5. **What's the difference between a pet-quality and breeding-rights Maine Coon?** *(pricing/product type)*
   Pet-quality kittens are desexed before going to their new home and priced for companionship; breeding-rights kittens are sold intact with full papers for registered breeding programs, at a higher price reflecting their pedigree value. Ask our concierge which is right for you via [Contact](/contact/).

6. **Are Maine Coons good family pets?** *(product type)*
   Yes — Maine Coons are known for being affectionate, patient with children, and generally good with dogs and other cats when socialised early. Read our full [temperament guide](/blog/are-maine-coons-good-family-pets/) for specifics.

7. **How big do Maine Coons get?** *(product type)*
   Adult males typically reach 8–13kg, adult females 5.5–8kg, with a body length up to 120cm nose to tail — among the largest domestic cat breeds. They take 3–5 years to reach full size. See our [size guide](/blog/how-big-do-maine-coons-get/).

- **Are Maine Coons good pets for families?** Yes — they're widely regarded as one of the most family-friendly large cat breeds, known for patience with children and a calm, sociable temperament. Full detail in our [temperament guide](/blog/are-maine-coons-good-family-pets/).

### Theme: Care, Grooming & Health
**Keywords covered:** maine coon grooming (50) · maine coon cat grooming (50) · grooming a maine coon (50) · do you have to bathe maine coon cats · maine coon groomer (40)

- **How often should I groom a Maine Coon?** At least weekly with a slicker brush and undercoat rake to prevent matting in their long double coat — more often during seasonal shedding. Full routine in our [grooming guide](/blog/maine-coon-grooming-guide/), and shop our [Royal Grooming Suite](/shop/royal-velvet-maine-coon-grooming-suite/).
- **Do Maine Coons need regular baths?** Not usually — their coat is semi-water-resistant and self-maintaining with regular brushing, though an occasional bath helps during heavy shedding seasons.
- **Are Maine Coons prone to health problems?** Like all pedigree breeds they carry known genetic risks — specifically HCM, SMA, and PKDef — which is exactly why DNA testing of breeding parents matters. Read our [DNA testing explainer](/blog/understanding-maine-coon-dna-testing-hcm-sma-pkdef/).

### Theme: Behaviour Myths & Safety
**Keywords covered:** are maine coon cats dangerous · are maine coon cats aggressive · can a maine coon kill a dog · are maine coon cats wild

- **Are Maine Coons dangerous?** No. Despite their large size, Maine Coons are consistently ranked among the gentlest, least aggressive cat breeds — "gentle giant" is a genuine description, not marketing. Full myth-by-myth breakdown in [Maine Coon Myths Debunked](/blog/maine-coon-myths-debunked/).
- **Are Maine Coons wild cats or a wild hybrid?** No — despite size and tufted ears suggesting a wildcat cross, Maine Coons are a fully domestic breed with no wildcat ancestry; the appearance comes from natural adaptation to cold New England winters.

### Theme: Breed Identification & History
**Keywords covered:** how do i know if my cat is maine coon · could my cat be part maine coon · how did the maine coon originate · what's a maine coon cat (480) · maine coon breed of cat (70)

- **How do I know if my cat is part Maine Coon?** Look for a squared muzzle, tufted lynx-like ears, a long ruff, and a body that keeps growing well past a year old — but the only certain way to confirm ancestry is a genetic breed test, since appearance alone can be misleading. See our [breed guide](/blog/complete-maine-coon-breed-guide/).
- **Where did the Maine Coon breed originate?** In the US state of Maine, most likely from long-haired cats brought by early sailors and settlers crossing with local domestic cats, naturally selected over generations for a thick weatherproof coat. Full history in our [breed guide](/blog/complete-maine-coon-breed-guide/).
