# Product & Content Gap Analysis — Cosy Cats Cattery

Scanned all clusters in `keyword-cluster.txt` against the site's existing pages (10 kitten listings, 3 merchandise products, 8 core pages). **No brand gaps found** — this dataset is single-niche (Maine Coons generally), not a multi-brand retail category, so the "competitor brand with no matching page" pattern from the skill's brand-gap check doesn't apply here. Two genuine category-level content gaps and one real regional opportunity were found instead.

---

## Gap 1 — Nutrition/Food Product Has Zero Keyword Coverage

- **Product type:** Large-breed/giant-breed cat nutrition (raw or holistic diet)
- **Existing page:** `/shop/holistic-giant-breed-vitality-pack/`
- **Problem:** None of the 5 keyword exports contain a single row for cat food, diet, or nutrition search terms — not "maine coon food," "large breed cat food," "raw cat food australia," nothing. This product currently has **no real keyword backing at all** in this research.
- **Demand signal:** Unknown — cannot estimate without new data.
- **Priority:** T1 action item, but the action is *research*, not content: **pull a dedicated keyword export** for terms like `maine coon food`, `large breed kitten food australia`, `raw diet for cats`, `giant breed cat nutrition`, `kitten food for maine coon` before writing any more SEO copy for this page. Until then, do not represent this page as keyword-optimised.

## Gap 2 — Cat Furniture Category Has Only One Real Keyword

- **Product type:** Heavy-duty cat trees/towers/scratching furniture for large-breed cats
- **Existing pages:** `/shop/monarch-grand-chateau-cat-castle/`, `/wholesale/`
- **Problem:** Only `maine coon cat tower` (Vol 70, KD 9) exists in the dataset for this entire category. That's enough to justify keeping the keyword as primary, but not enough to build a confident supporting-keyword set (5 required per page) from real data alone.
- **Demand signal:** 70/mo confirmed; likely undercounted since general "cat tree/scratching post" searches weren't part of this Maine-Coon-focused research pull.
- **Suggested category:** Could support a dedicated `/shop/luxury-care/` or `/accessories/` category page once more data exists, consolidating the cat tower + grooming products under one SEO-supported hub rather than two isolated product pages.
- **Priority:** T2. **Recommended follow-up pull:** `cat tree australia`, `large cat scratching post`, `heavy duty cat tower`, `cat furniture for big cats`.

## Gap 3 — Wholesale/Trade Page Has No B2B Keyword Data

- **Product type:** B2B bulk supply (timber cat furniture + nutrition) to vet clinics, feline hotels, other catteries
- **Existing page:** `/wholesale/`
- **Problem:** No wholesale/trade/bulk-supply search terms appear anywhere in the 5 exports — this research was entirely consumer-side (people searching to buy one kitten or one product), not B2B.
- **Priority:** T3 (lowest — wholesale is a small side channel for this business, not core revenue). **Recommended follow-up pull if pursued seriously:** `wholesale cat furniture australia`, `bulk pet supplies trade account`, `cattery supplies wholesale`.

## Opportunity 1 — Regional Delivery Content (Real Demand, No Dedicated Page Yet)

- **Signal:** `maine coon kittens for sale melbourne` (390, KD9) and `maine coon breeders sydney` (70, KD1) both show real, measurable demand from buyers outside Canberra.
- **Recommendation:** **Do not build fake "Melbourne branch" or "Sydney office" location pages** — Cosy Cats Cattery has one physical location (Canberra) and ships nationwide; claiming local presence in cities where the business has no premises would be a false signal (and risks a Google Business Profile / local-pack policy violation if ever extended that far).
- **Honest alternative already planned:** the Contact page cluster (`contact-regional` in `keyword-cluster.txt`) targets these terms legitimately — answering "do you deliver to Sydney/Melbourne" as a shipping/delivery FAQ, which is both truthful and captures the search intent. This is the correct-scale response to real demand without overclaiming presence.
- **Priority:** T1 — already assigned to `/contact/` in the keyword map; no new page needed, just make sure the delivery-by-city content is genuinely on that page when it's next updated.

## Opportunity 2 — "Maine Coon Cattery" as a Dedicated Trust/Brand Anchor

- **Signal:** `maine coon cattery` (50, KD10) is a low-volume but extremely high-relevance exact match — it's literally the business category name.
- **Recommendation:** Already assigned as a supporting keyword on Homepage and About. No new page needed — just ensure the phrase "Maine Coon cattery" appears naturally and prominently (not just "cattery" alone) in both pages' first 150 words, since this is also the strongest entity-coverage signal available for AI/GEO visibility.
- **Priority:** T1, zero-cost (copy tweak only, not a new build).
